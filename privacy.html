Railway Logbook Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 September 2026

Railway Logbook is a personal duty-record application. Most app data is stored locally in the user’s browser. The developer does not operate a separate server that collects duty records.

Google account access

If the user chooses to connect Google, Railway Logbook requests access needed to create and update the user’s own Google Drive backup files and linked Google Sheet. Google data is used only to perform features initiated by the user.

Data storage and retention

Local records remain in the browser until the user deletes them or clears browser data. Drive backups and Sheet records remain in the user’s Google account until the user deletes them. Railway Logbook does not automatically delete Google Drive backups.

Sharing

Railway Logbook does not sell personal information or share duty records with advertisers. Data is transmitted to Google only when the user enables Google backup or Sheet synchronization.

User control

Users can disconnect Google access inside Settings, revoke access from their Google Account permissions, export a local backup, or delete stored data.
